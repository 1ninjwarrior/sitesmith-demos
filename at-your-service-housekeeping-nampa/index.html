About At Your Service Housekeeping

At Your Service Housekeeping Co is a locally owned LLC in Nampa. Co-owner Kaylie Longhurst and member manager Maurenn L. Vogrig run it. We see housekeeping as more than wiping counters. Our goal is "redefining housekeeping and project management one property at a time." That's why we offer organizing, laundry, errand running and home planning along with the cleaning. We work in homes and light commercial spaces across Canyon and Ada counties.
