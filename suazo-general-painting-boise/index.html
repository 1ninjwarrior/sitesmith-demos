About

Why homeowners call us

Suazo General Painting is a local Boise painting company. We paint interiors and exteriors for homes and businesses, and we paint new construction too. Our recent work includes a new build in Kuna with four bedrooms and three bathrooms, a downtown Boise home we repainted from red to white, and a two-color house in Eagle with an accent door. If you have a project in mind, give us a call and we'll come out and give you a free estimate.
