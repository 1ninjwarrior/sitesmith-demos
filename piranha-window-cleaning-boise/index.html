About Piranha Window Cleaning

Piranha Advanced Window Cleaning is locally owned and operated, with one simple promise in its name: clean to the bone. We wash windows, clear gutters and pressure wash homes across the Treasure Valley and up into McCall, Sun Valley and Stanley.

  • Locally owned and operated

    An Idaho company, not a national franchise call center.

  • Valley to the mountains

    Boise, Meridian, Nampa and Caldwell, plus McCall, Sun Valley and Stanley.

  • One call, three services

    Windows, gutters and pressure washing handled together.
