About FES Handyman

FES Handyman is a Boise handyman business that takes on pretty much everything handyman-wise. That covers fixing a leaky faucet, laying new tile, painting a room, and putting down weed barrier in the yard. Neighbors on Nextdoor named us a Neighborhood Favorite in 2024 and again in 2025. We want good work to be easy to afford, so we give free estimates, offer payment plans, and take money off for veterans, seniors, people with disabilities and returning customers. We also do pro bono work for people who qualify.

  • A Neighborhood Favorite

    Boise neighbors on Nextdoor named FES Handyman a Neighborhood Favorite in both 2024 and 2025.

  • Fair and flexible on cost

    Estimates are free, payment plans are available, and veterans, seniors, people with disabilities and returning customers get a discount.

  • Open on weekends too

    We're available Monday to Friday 9am–5pm and Saturday and Sunday 11am–3pm, so you don't have to take time off work.
